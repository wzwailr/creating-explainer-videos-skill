# AI 原理一手资料调研

Use original papers, official documentation, official repositories, model cards, and standards for mechanism facts. Community material may reveal confusing cases or failure symptoms, but does not establish the mechanism by itself.

For every key claim record the source, source type, verification date, any teaching simplification, and the planned visual expression. Label invented example numbers as teaching examples, never benchmarks. Recheck time-sensitive APIs, models, versions, prices, and performance claims immediately before scripting.
