# Primary-source research

Use primary records, original research, official documentation, standards, repositories, and authoritative datasets for mechanism facts. Community material may reveal confusing cases or failure symptoms, but does not establish a mechanism by itself.

For every key claim record the source, source type, verification date, teaching simplification, and planned visual expression. Label invented numbers as teaching examples rather than measurements. Recheck time-sensitive APIs, products, standards, prices, and performance claims immediately before scripting.
